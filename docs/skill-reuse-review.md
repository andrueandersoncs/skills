# Skill reuse review

Started 2026-09-04. Append-only: new visits, comparisons, corrections, and conclusions go at the end; earlier entries remain unchanged.

## Scope and method

- Review every `SKILL.md` under `skills/`, including nested workflows and verbatim upstream snapshots, plus supporting references and executable assets relevant to reuse or conflicting instructions.
- Assume every skill is always available. Prefer a precise link to an existing owner over copying its procedure. Availability does not require loading every skill into every task.
- Deliver an audit, not edits to the skills. Each actionable finding identifies source evidence, the existing reuse target, and the smallest proposed deletion, cross-reference, or conflict resolution.
- Distinguish live instructions from examples and archived sources. Preserve useful domain-specific checks and upstream attribution; repeated subject matter alone does not justify consolidation.
- Priority: **High** changes task ownership or can cause an incorrect result; **Medium** removes duplicated procedure or resolves drift; **Low** improves navigation without changing behavior.
- Software-law framing: use one authority for shared knowledge (DRY), retain the smallest complete working design (Gall), and assess observable behavior before changing contracts (Hyrum). These are decision prompts, not evidence by themselves; see [software-laws](../skills/help/references/software-laws/SKILL.md).
- This is a reference-oriented audit for the skill maintainer. Findings cite repository-relative source links and line ranges. Recommendations remain provisional until the cross-skill comparison and independent review appended at the end.
