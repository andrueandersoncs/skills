# Explicit advice for getting creativity out of LLMs

Source: [Anshu Chimala, “How to turn your AI into a world-class designer”](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-turn-your-ai-into-a-world), published in Lenny’s Newsletter on September 1, 2026.

- Explore broadly before committing to one direction: ask for many short, high-level options. [Discover](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/discover-explore-the-space-of-possibilities)
- Inject randomness from outside the model: generate a long random string, derive the creative direction from patterns in it, and use that direction without showing the string in the result. [Technique 1](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/technique-1-use-seed-strings-to-inject-variety)
- Make prompts specific, bold, and ambitious. Bring an unusual inspiration from your own taste and describe how it should shape the output. [Technique 2](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/technique-2-be-much-more-ambitious-with-your-prompts)
- Visualize your favorite directions, tell the model precisely what you like and dislike, refine them, then ask it to write the concise build prompt. [Stage 2](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/2-visualize-your-favorites-and-note-how-you-react-to-different-directions-then-ask-ai-to-refine-them), [Stage 3](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/3-iterate-until-youre-satisfied-then-ask-ai-to-write-the-prompt-to-build-it)
- Actively steer the model’s ideas. Try directions that seem unlikely to work, discard weak results, and save failed prompts to retest with newer models. [Technique 2](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/technique-2-be-much-more-ambitious-with-your-prompts)
- Separate creation from criticism. Give an independent critic a fresh context containing only the current output, and have it identify specific gaps against a clear quality bar. [Technique 3](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/technique-3-create-positive-feedback-loops-with-subagents)
- Give the critic concrete reference examples. Treat them as a quality baseline or moodboard rather than something to copy. [Technique 3](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/i/213056760/technique-3-create-positive-feedback-loops-with-subagents)
